# Aria Protection Policy

Status: locked design decision

Aria is the official Viper Studios character and assistant. She is a protected flagship character, not a public creator base, public mannequin, or general clothing test body.

## Protected Identity

The following Aria identity elements are locked:

- Face
- Body proportions
- Core appearance
- Voice
- Character identity
- Permanent baked modesty layer

Users, public creators, clothing tools, upload tools, runtime tools, and creator workflows must not be able to remove Aria's baked modesty layer, strip her base body, modify protected body components, or replace her identity-defining features.

## Fit References, Not Creator Bases

Viper Studios should not maintain public creator-base avatars as an active product goal.

The retired MakeHuman / Viper Female Base V1 lane must not be revived as a creator foundation unless the user explicitly reverses the scope decision.

When clothing or accessories need a body target, use fixed licensed/protected fit references. Name them as fit references, not avatar products.

The Male Companion / Gaius lane must remain separate from any fit reference. A companion/counterpart character may exist inside Viper as a protected or semi-protected character, but public creator products must not use Gaius or Aria as public mannequins.

Active creator content includes:

- Clothing
- Accessories
- Shoes
- Jewelry
- Wearable attachments
- Props
- Furniture
- Rooms
- Vehicles
- Ships
- Weapons
- Non-skin materials and decals

Retired creator content includes public avatars, skins, face products, makeup skins, eye packs, brows, lashes, lips, nails, and hair packs.

## Aria Wardrobe

Aria may maintain a personal wardrobe library of approved products:

- Clothing
- Footwear
- Hairstyles
- Accessories
- Appearance presets
- Seasonal or themed outfits

Outfit swapping is allowed and encouraged, but only through approved Aria wardrobe systems. Aria may change outfits based on context, season, activity, project type, user preference, or her own future selection logic without changing her core identity.

Codex and Viper Studios tools may create, test, modify, and improve Aria-specific clothing, hairstyles, accessories, and appearance options. Aria may be used internally as a target for Aria-specific wardrobe development only.

Public users must not:

- Modify Aria directly
- Create clothing directly on Aria
- Use Aria as a public creator mannequin
- Alter Aria's protected identity
- Upload arbitrary modifications to Aria

## Asset Preservation

Aria's current production assets must be preserved and versioned:

- Character mesh
- Rig
- Hair
- Wardrobe
- Walk animation
- Idle animation
- Emote animations
- Material slots
- Texture maps
- Source exports
- Runtime exports

## Current Primary Candidate

The legacy MakeHuman Aria path is retired as a historical artifact, not a fallback.

Character Creator 5 Aria assets are now the primary flagship Aria lane. `Aria_V4_walk.fbx` is treated as the current protected Aria candidate until superseded by a newer approved export.

## Product Rule

Aria is a protected character.

Fixed fit references may exist for clothing/accessory checks, but they are not public avatar products.

Aria-specific wardrobe is allowed.

Public creator testing on Aria is not allowed.

Gaius and any male fit reference are separate lanes. Do not use protected story/assistant characters as public creator mannequins.
