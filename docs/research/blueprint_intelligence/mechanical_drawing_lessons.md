# Mechanical Drawing Lessons

## Level 14: Mechanical / Industrial Drawings
Mechanical drawings focus on the fabrication and machining of individual solid parts.

### Key Concepts
- **Machined Components**: Parts fabricated by cutting material away (milling, turning). Look for surface finish symbols (e.g., √) denoting required smoothness.
- **Sheet Metal**: Parts fabricated by bending flat stock. Drawings often show the folded 3D state and a "Flat Pattern" state indicating bend lines and bend radii.
- **Weldments**: Assemblies of parts permanently joined by welding. Look for welding symbols on leader lines indicating weld type (fillet, groove), size, and location (arrow side, other side).
- **Standard Hardware**: Gears, bearings, flanges, and shafts often use standardized proportions.

### HELIOS/HAL Directives:
- Connect mechanical drawing properties directly to hard-surface manufacturing systems.
- For sheet metal, understand that the material thickness is constant.
- For weldments, model the individual components first, then define the welded assembly relationship.
