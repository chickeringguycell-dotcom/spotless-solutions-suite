# Piping Drawing Lessons

## Level 15: Piping & P&ID Intelligence
Piping drawings describe fluid flow and control systems.

### Key Concepts
- **P&ID (Piping and Instrumentation Diagram)**: A schematic showing the piping process flow together with installed equipment and instrumentation. Not drawn to scale.
- **Isometric Drawings (Isos)**: A 3D representation (usually drawn on a 30-degree isometric grid) of physical pipe routing, including pipe lengths, fittings, valves, and weld locations.
- **Equipment Tags**: Identifiers (e.g., P-101 for a pump, V-205 for a valve) linking symbols to equipment specifications.
- **Line Numbers**: Identifiers indicating pipe size, fluid service, material class, and sequence.

### HELIOS/HAL Directives:
- Differentiate between P&ID (logical flow) and Isometrics (physical routing).
- Do not attempt to build 3D physical pipe from a P&ID. Use the P&ID to understand the system logic and component requirements.
- Use Isometric drawings to manufacture 3D pipe routing. Note that Isos are often not to scale physically, but the dimension labels are exact and govern the 3D model.
