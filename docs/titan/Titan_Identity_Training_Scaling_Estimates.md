# Titan Identity Training Scaling Estimates

## Phase 11 — Scaling Decision
**Status**: `BLOCKED`

The determination of whether the clean synthetic identity encoder feasibility is verified (A), requires more poses (B), fails to generalize (C), or requires real-face data (G) is entirely dependent on the future execution of Encoder Stage C and Adapter Stage C on the Viper Synthetic Identity Dataset V0. 

No decision can be mathematically supported until the V0 dataset is generated and trained.
