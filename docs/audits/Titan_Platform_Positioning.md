## CLASSIFICATION
**RESEARCH PIPELINE / PARTIAL ENGINE UNDER DEVELOPMENT**

Titan is not ready to serve as a completely automated, hands-off commercial vertical slice. It is a research pipeline that produces usable assets locally but lacks the identity consistency and automated QA required to be the platform's flawless default generator. It must be positioned as a *Module* within the platform, not the *entirety* of the platform roadmap.
