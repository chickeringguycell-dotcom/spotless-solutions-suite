# Viper Studios Google Accelerator Audit
**Date:** 2026-07-20

## 1. Google Stitch
- **Official Product Name:** Google Stitch
- **Official Provider:** Google
- **Current Availability:** Experimental / Labs
- **Status:** Free / Usage-limited during experimental phase (subject to Labs quotas)
- **Account Requirement:** Google Account (Workspace/Labs)
- **Region Restrictions:** Varies by Google Labs rollout
- **Inputs and Outputs:** 
  - *Inputs:* Text prompts, URLs. 
  - *Outputs:* UI prototypes.
- **Export Formats:** React, HTML, CSS, Tailwind, design-file export, code-import, SDK, MCP, and DESIGN.md capabilities (recorded only when officially verified by Google).
- **API Availability:** UNCONFIRMED.
- **Source-Code Availability:** N/A for core model; exported code is standard readable frontend code.
- **Commercial-Use Terms:** Google Labs Terms of Service (experimental).
- **Data-Retention & Privacy:** Subject to Google Labs terms. Do not upload proprietary backend IP.
- **Antigravity Compatibility:** Export or handoff capabilities to Antigravity are recorded only when officially verified. Exported work must be preserved as ordinary Viper-owned source files.
- **Can export be incorporated into Viper Studios?** Yes, provided no proprietary hooks or vendor dependencies are allowed into production without review.
- **Temporary production use:** N/A.
- **Vendor Lock-in:** Low (exports raw code).
- **Viper Usefulness Classification:** FIRST CONTROLLED EXTERNAL ACCELERATOR AFTER P0 BUILD REPAIR.
